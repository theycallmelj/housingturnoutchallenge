## Description

A survey-based index of local residential land-use regulation (approval steps, density restrictions, open-space requirements, impact fees, approval delays), standardized across jurisdictions.

## Known limitations

- A **survey of planners**: self-reported, with nonresponse bias. Coverage skews suburban, and many rural places and some big cities are missing.
- Snapshots in 2006 and 2018 only. It does not capture post-2018 state reforms.
- An index, not specific rules. It can't tell us, for example, a jurisdiction's minimum ADU lot size.
